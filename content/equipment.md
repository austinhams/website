Club-owned equipment carries a numbered asset tag. Scanning the QR code on a tag
opens that item's record in the club's [equipment inventory](https://gear.austinhams.org/).

If you scanned a tag and landed here, the QR code may be damaged or the tag
number may not be registered yet. You can browse the full inventory or
check out equipment at
[gear.austinhams.org](https://gear.austinhams.org/), or
[get in touch](/contact/) and we'll sort it out.
