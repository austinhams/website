Usage guidelines

You're welcome to use our logo to refer to the Austin Amateur Radio Club — in news coverage, on event materials, or to link to us. Following these guidelines keeps it looking right everywhere it appears.

Do

  • Use the original files from this page.
  • Use the full-color logo on light backgrounds and the reversed or white logo on dark backgrounds.
  • Use the black or white logo when printing in a single color.
  • Scale the logo proportionally so it keeps its original shape.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least equal to the height of the “Est. 1916” text.
  • Keep the logo at least 1 inch (or 100 pixels) wide so “Austin Amateur Radio Club” stays legible.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate, or crop the logo.
  • Change its colors or substitute other colors.
  • Add effects such as drop shadows, outlines, glows, or gradients.
  • Rearrange, remove, or redraw any part of the logo.
  • Place it on busy backgrounds or photos where it's hard to read.
  • Combine it with other logos or text to create a new mark.
  • Use it in a way that implies AARC sponsors or endorses a product, service, event, or organization without our written permission.
  • Use it on merchandise or anything offered for sale without our written permission.
