Member Recognition

{{ .Title }}

Honoring AARC members whose dedication and service have made a lasting impact on the amateur radio community.

Each year, the Austin Amateur Radio Club presents the Ham of the Year Award to an individual whose exceptional dedication, service, and spirit have made a lasting impact on the amateur radio community. The award recognizes contributions that go above and beyond in advancing the hobby, supporting club activities, promoting emergency preparedness, and strengthening our shared mission as amateur radio operators.
